Before setup

Check compatibility first

WL1 requires factory wired Apple CarPlay through USB. Wireless-only CarPlay is not compatible. The Apple list is a good starting point, but it includes both wired and wireless CarPlay vehicles.

Use Apple's CarPlay vehicle list

Apple's list shows vehicles that support CarPlay, but it does not confirm whether your exact vehicle supports wired CarPlay through USB. WL1 does not convert wireless-only CarPlay, so test your vehicle by connecting your iPhone with a USB data cable.

Quick test

Confirm wired CarPlay before pairing WL1

Start the vehicle, wait for the head unit to load, connect your iPhone with a USB data cable, and confirm CarPlay starts from that port. If CarPlay only works wirelessly, WL1 cannot convert that connection.

Pre-flight checklist

Before you start

These steps prevent the most common pairing mistakes and reduce the chance of connecting to the wrong Bluetooth or Wi-Fi target.

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on

Keep both enabled on your iPhone before starting setup.

Use the CarPlay port

Wait for the head unit to load, then plug WL1 into the wired CarPlay USB port.

Do not join Wi-Fi manually

Do not manually connect to BW-smartBox-xxxx Wi-Fi during normal setup.

First-time setup

Pair once through iPhone CarPlay settings

After pairing, Wi-Fi connects automatically for the CarPlay data connection. No separate app or Wi-Fi password is needed during normal setup.

Plug in WL1

Connect the adapter to the vehicle USB port that supports wired CarPlay.

Open CarPlay

On iPhone, go to Settings > General > CarPlay.

Choose device

Under Other Cars, tap BW-smartBox-xxxx.

Pair and connect

Tap Pair, then tap Connect. CarPlay should appear after about 15 to 20 seconds.

Open CarPlay

Go to iPhone CarPlay settings.

Select WL1

Tap the BW-smartBox device.

Pair

Accept the Bluetooth pairing request.

Connect

Allow CarPlay to connect wirelessly.

Daily use

Reconnect automatically after first-time setup

Before entering the vehicle, keep iPhone Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on. Start the vehicle and wait for the head unit to load. WL1 should reconnect automatically, with timing varying by vehicle system.

Self-service help

Troubleshooting by symptom

Start with the symptom that matches what you see. If wired CarPlay does not work with a USB cable, resolve that vehicle issue before using WL1.

CarPlay does not start or USB device is not recognized
  1. Confirm wired CarPlay works by connecting your iPhone with a USB data cable.
  2. Make sure WL1 is plugged into the same USB port used for wired CarPlay.
  3. Turn off iPhone Bluetooth, unplug WL1, reconnect it, then pair again.
iPhone cannot find BW-smartBox-xxxx
  1. Check that the adapter indicator lights briefly when connected.
  2. Try another iPhone if possible.
  3. If only one phone cannot find WL1, reset network settings on that phone as a last resort.
Auto reconnect does not work
  1. Turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi before starting the vehicle.
  2. Disconnect other auto-connected Bluetooth accessories if they interfere.
  3. Remove BW-smartBox-xxxx from Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and CarPlay settings, then pair again.
No sound, microphone issue, calls issue, or Siri issue

Disconnect other Bluetooth audio devices such as earbuds or watches. During calls, confirm that audio output is set to CarPlay.

Connection drops or reconnect is slow

Disable other in-vehicle Wi-Fi devices if possible, keep the iPhone close to WL1, restart the iPhone and adapter, then test again.

Can I use another Wi-Fi network while using WL1?

No. Wireless CarPlay uses iPhone Wi-Fi for the adapter connection. Mobile data can still be used for internet access.

Does WL1 support YouTube or Netflix on the vehicle display?

No. WL1 supports Wireless CarPlay and does not support YouTube, Netflix, or similar video playback on the vehicle display.

Is my BMW compatible with WL1?

Many BMW CarPlay systems are wireless-only and do not support CarPlay through USB, so WL1 may not be compatible. Before using WL1, test your BMW by connecting an iPhone with a USB data cable and confirming wired CarPlay starts from that USB port.

Is my Tesla compatible with WL1?

WL1 requires factory wired CarPlay through USB. Current Tesla vehicles are not known to provide factory wired CarPlay, so WL1 should not be used for Tesla unless your vehicle has a confirmed wired CarPlay USB port.

Firmware update

Update only when troubleshooting requires it

If WL1 is working normally, firmware update is optional. During update, keep stable power and do not unplug the adapter.

1. Open update page

Use Safari on iPhone and open 192.168.8.1.

2. Check build number

Review the system information screen before updating.

3. Tap Update

Start online update only if a new version is available.

4. Pair again if needed

Remove BW-smartBox-xxxx from CarPlay, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi settings, then pair again.
